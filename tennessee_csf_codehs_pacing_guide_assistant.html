Curricular Gap Diagnostics & Interactive Solutions

The Tennessee Computer Science Foundations Standards (May 2025 Update) introduce several unique pathways and hardware competencies that standard coding modules do not thoroughly cover. This pacing guide details specific remedies for your classroom:

🔌 1. Electronics & Ohm's Law (2.1 - 2.2)

CodeHS teaches binary logic gates conceptually, but lacks interactive physics testing. We supplement this by integrating **Tinkercad Circuits** virtual breadboards, enabling students to construct virtual circuits and measure voltage/amperage changes safely.

🤖 2. Ethical AI (Standard 13.2)

The updated standards emphasize Bias, Privacy, and Ethical AI. We inject custom **Google Teachable Machine** workflows into Week 15 so students train simple ML models and directly observe bias errors and dataset limitations.

🖥️ 3. Command Line Blocks (16.3)

School firewalls and Chrome OS machines block access to Windows CMD or macOS Terminal. Students can complete this standard by utilizing the **integrated safe CLI sandbox** built right into this application (accessible via the top menu).
